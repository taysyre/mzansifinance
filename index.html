Good to know

A few things
you might be wondering.

Not sure where to start? That's completely okay. Here are a few answers to help.

What happens when I get in touch?

We'll start by listening to what's going on and asking a few questions to understand your situation. From there, we can talk through possible next steps, with no pressure to decide on the spot.

Is my conversation confidential?

We treat your information with care and respect. Before sharing sensitive details, ask us how your information is handled and what privacy protections apply.

Can you guarantee my debt will be removed?

No responsible service can promise a specific outcome without understanding your circumstances. We'll explain what options may be available and what each one involves.

Do I need to have all my documents ready?

No. You can begin with a conversation. If documents would help later, we'll let you know what to gather and why.
